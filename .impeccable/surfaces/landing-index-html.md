# Landing · lifeplanner

Surface: `landing/index.html` — **Persuade**. A stranger must understand the offer, believe the mechanism, and open the real demo dashboard.

Audience: people who have tried todo lists and feel that completion does not equal progress. Job: decide whether this tool is worth ten minutes a week. Proof: the generated demo dashboard from the synthetic seed, never invented users or metrics.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a personal calibration statement, not a SaaS landing. It refuses the category default of a friendly productivity hero and shows a settlement with a verdict.

OWN-WORLD: The existing dashboard's audit language, raised into a ledger: cool paper ground, hairline rules, ink-black primary actions, blue data accent, red overconfidence signal, Noto Serif CJK display, system sans body, monospaced numerals. No gradients, glass, icon-card grids, or rounded dashboard tiles.

STORY: The visitor sees that completion is not the same as calibration, learns the three-step loop, inspects a real dashboard, and can run the demo in three minutes or open the sample dashboard immediately.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, a two-line serif verdict “计划会骗你。结算不会。” with one paragraph, two actions, and a local-first line. Right, a bordered settlement sheet with a prediction row, a reliability chart, a red “系统性高估” stamp, and an 事前/结算后 toggle. The sheet is the hero; no metric cards.

FORM: Ledger statement. Existing world extended; no new identity roll. Signature interaction: the settlement toggle flips the sheet between the prediction and its outcome.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
